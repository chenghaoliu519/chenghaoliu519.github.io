The idea

Adapt the draft to
realized throughput.

A self-draft skips parts of the target model to propose tokens cheaply. But high draft acceptance alone does not guarantee fast decoding: each configuration also changes the cost of drafting and verification.

SPIRIT measures committed tokens per decode second, first finds an effective skip ratio, and then refines the layer pattern. It searches while producing output and carries useful profiles across recurring workloads.

No retraining. No offline profiling. No separately trained draft.
