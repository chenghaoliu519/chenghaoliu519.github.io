The method

Search while decoding. Reuse when workloads recur.

Profile reuse details
Compare each profile with its own history.
d(p) = |zS| + ω|zα|

Each z is the head measurement’s deviation from that profile’s historical mean, divided by its historical standard deviation. Lower d indicates a closer fit.

A switch must pass all three checks.
  • A closer fit to the candidate’s own history
  • Higher relative probe merit
  • Safe throughput

Request-head matching uses raw throughput and acceptance. The calibrated score Qw guides within-request search.
