# Conference and affiliation marks

- **NeurIPS:** unchanged navigation logo from the [official NeurIPS website](https://neurips.cc/static/core/img/neurips-navbar-logo.svg). The [official media kit](https://neurips.cc/public/MediaKit) provides the corresponding full logo.
- **UTA:** vector export of the primary logo used in the author-supplied SPIRIT poster. Converted from its vector PDF with `pdftocairo -svg`; geometry and colors are preserved. Source PDF SHA-256: `ccbc826d1d8bdd3560084ad584e1c0c9f96bc5e423d0724897f21c0e9b5fb8e3`. See UTA's [logo and signature system](https://www.uta.edu/administration/mme/brand-standards/logo-design-identity/logo-signature-system).

The marks identify the paper's conference and author affiliation. Their designs
remain the property of their respective organizations. Keep their proportions
and original colors; the UTA logo uses a white surface in both themes.
